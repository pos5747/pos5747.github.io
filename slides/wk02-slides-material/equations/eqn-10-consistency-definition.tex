\textbf{Consistency.} Let $\hat{\theta}_N$ be an estimator of $\theta$ based on a sample of size $N$. Say that $\hat{\theta}_N$ is a \textbf{consistent} estimator for $\theta$ if $\lim_{N \to \infty} \Pr \left( |\hat{\theta}_N - \theta| \ge \varepsilon \right) = 0$ for every $\varepsilon > 0$.

\vspace{4mm}

\textbf{Consistency of ML Estimators.} Suppose an ML estimator $\hat{\theta}$ of $\theta$. Under certain \textit{regularity conditions}, $\hat{\theta}$ is a consistent estimator of $\theta$.
