\textbf{Maximum Likelihood Estimator.} Suppose we have iid samples $x_1, x_2, ..., x_N$ from pdf or pmf $f(x; \theta)$. Then the joint density/probability is $f(x; \theta) = \prod_{i = 1}^N f(x_i; \theta)$ and $\log L(\theta) = \sum_{i = 1}^N \log \left[ f(x_i; \theta) \right]$. The ML estimator $\hat{\theta}$ of $\theta$ is $\arg \max \log L(\theta)$.
