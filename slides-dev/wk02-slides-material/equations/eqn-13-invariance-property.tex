\textbf{Invariance Property.} Suppose an ML estimator $\hat{\theta}$ of $\theta$ and a quantity of interest $\tau = \tau(\theta)$ for any function $\tau$. The ML estimate $\hat{\tau}$ of $\tau = \tau(\theta)$ is $\tau(\hat{\theta})$.
