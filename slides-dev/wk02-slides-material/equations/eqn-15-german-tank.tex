Suppose a discrete uniform distribution from $0$ to $K$. The pmf is $f(x; K) = \frac{1}{K + 1}, \quad x \in \{0, 1, \ldots, K\}.$
Suppose I have a sample of size 3 from the distribution: 276, 159, and 912.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Find the ML estimate of $K$. \textit{Hint: The log-likelihood is discontinuous, so the usual optimization routine might mislead you. But the maximum is immediately apparent once you write out the likelihood. A verbal argument is okay, too!}

\item Find the method of moments estimate of $K$. \textit{Hint: The mean of this uniform distribution is $\tfrac{K}{2}$. Set the sample mean equal to the model mean (i.e., $\tfrac{K}{2} = \operatorname{avg}(x)$) and solve.}

\item Discuss any problems you notice with each estimator.
\end{enumerate}
